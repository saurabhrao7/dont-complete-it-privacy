Privacy Policy for Don't Complete It

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Don't Complete It ("the Game"), developed by Saurabh Rao, handles information when you use the Game.

1. Information We Collect

Don't Complete It does not require you to create an account and does not directly collect your name, email address, phone number, physical address, contacts, photos, videos, messages, calendar information, health information, or precise location.

Game progress and statistics may be stored locally on your device. This can include information such as scores, wins, losses, streaks, puzzle progress, and daily-puzzle status.

2. Advertising

Don't Complete It uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertising, including banner advertisements and rewarded advertisements.

Google Mobile Ads may collect and process information such as device or advertising identifiers, diagnostic information, and information about interactions with advertisements. This information may be used for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance.

Google's handling of information is governed by Google's privacy policy. For more information, please visit Google's Privacy Policy .

3. Data Security

Information transmitted by the advertising services used by the Game is transmitted using secure connections where supported by those services.

4. Children's Privacy

Don't Complete It is a general-audience puzzle game and is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13.

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Game progress and statistics stored locally can be removed by removing the Game and its locally stored application data from your device.

Information handled by third-party services such as Google Mobile Ads is subject to those services' own retention and deletion policies.

6. Third-Party Services

The Game uses third-party services, including Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
